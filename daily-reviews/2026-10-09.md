## What actually happened

**09:42:00am ET** — PRME fired a genuine `LONG_ENTRY` at 3.67. The BUY order placed, but `pollOrderFill` timed out before confirming it FILLED (it had, just not within the 5-second poll window — confirmed later via order history: filled at **$3.66**, 13:42:11 UTC). Per the 2026-10-02 fix, the position was registered and `entry_placed` logged immediately regardless.

**Then the stop placement hit the same 417 `OPENAPI_OPEN_ORDER_HAS_BOX_ORDER` race the 10-02 fix was built around** — Webull won't accept a SELL stop against a BUY that hasn't confirmed FILLED yet. All 3 retries (`placeStopWithRetry`) failed within 4 seconds, exactly as designed, correctly triggering the fail-safe: `emergencyFlatten()`.

**`emergencyFlatten()` itself then hit the identical race** — its own market SELL order threw the same 417, because the entry still hadn't confirmed FILLED from Webull's side at that exact moment either. Unlike `placeStopWithRetry()`, `emergencyFlatten()` has **no retry logic of its own** — this is a real gap in the 10-02 fix, found here for the first time. The exception propagated all the way out of `enterLong()`, caught only by `main.ts`'s generic handler and logged as `[risk] enterLong failed for PRME: ...` — no further recovery was attempted that pass.

**The position stayed registered in memory** (the core 10-02 fix held — it wasn't silently lost) but with a `stopClientOrderId` pointing at an order that was never actually created. Every subsequent `onPriceUpdate` call for the rest of the session threw `417 Order not present` trying to replace a stop that didn't exist — dozens of identical errors logged from 9:42am through the 3:20pm close, with the position effectively un-manageable (no stop resting, no way for the normal code paths to flatten it) the entire time.

**At EOD**, `closeAllEndOfDay()` tried to cancel that same phantom stop first and also got `417 Order not present` — `closeOneEndOfDay()` calls `cancelOrderWithRetry` directly, unwrapped, so this exception aborted the whole close attempt for PRME: `[risk] EOD: failed to close PRME, will remain open`. The primary session ended with PRME **genuinely open at the broker**, no stop, no scripted flatten.

**It got closed anyway, by chance** — a redundant backup `schedule` run started at 3:20:28pm ET (8 seconds after the primary's own EOD attempt failed), ran its own `reconcile()`, found PRME (`cost=3.66, stopTracked=false`), and — since enough time had now passed for the original entry to be unambiguously FILLED at Webull — successfully sold it via a plain market order, filling at **$3.98**. A real, genuine `exit_filled` was logged inside that run.

**But that run's commit was lost.** Its `git pull --rebase origin main` hit a merge conflict in both `trades.jsonl` and `watchlist.json` against the primary session's own (failed, but still attempted) commit, and the whole job exited with an error — the same **pre-existing, previously-documented, never-structurally-fixed commit-race class of bug** from the 2026-09-01 and 2026-09-08 incidents in `CLAUDE.md`. The PRME exit never reached `origin/main`.

**Net result**: PRME was a real, clean **+$96.00 winner** (3.66 → 3.98, 300 shares) that is completely invisible in the repo's own trade history. Confirmed via `GET /openapi/trade/order/history` directly against the broker — this write-up is the only record of it.

## Two distinct bugs, not one

1. **`emergencyFlatten()` needs the same retry treatment as `placeStopWithRetry()`** — it's subject to the identical timing race (entry not yet confirmed FILLED), and currently has zero retries. This is a gap in the fix shipped 2026-10-02, three days before this incident.
2. **The commit-race data-loss issue from 09-01/09-08 is still open** and just cost a real, if modest, loss of financial record integrity (a genuine $96 win, silently gone). `CLAUDE.md` already flagged this as a structural TODO; this is a new, concrete data point for prioritizing it — e.g., retrying the rebase after re-fetching, or switching the commit step to a merge/append strategy that can't lose lines this way.
