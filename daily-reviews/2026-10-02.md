## Root cause: `enterLong()` can leave a real position completely unprotected and unlogged

At 9:31:00am ET, NVTS fired a genuine `LONG_ENTRY` signal at 12.59. `enterLong()` (`orderManager.ts:380`) placed the BUY market order successfully, then polled for fill confirmation (`pollOrderFill`) and **timed out without confirming FILLED status** — not itself unusual, logged as a warning, falling back to the signal price for stop-distance math. Execution continues past that fallback (it doesn't return early).

It then immediately tried to place the protective `STOP_LOSS` sell order using that fallback price — and Webull rejected it:

```
417 OPENAPI_OPEN_ORDER_HAS_BOX_ORDER: "Buy opening orders and sell opening
orders for the same stock or option cannot exist simultaneously."
```

This makes sense: the original BUY hadn't actually confirmed as filled yet (that's *why* the poll timed out), so Webull won't accept a SELL stop against a position that, from its side, doesn't exist yet. The stop-order POST call throws (confirmed via the stack trace in the run log, pointing at `orderManager.ts:479`), and **the function exits there** — before ever reaching `this.positions.set(...)` (line 491) or `logTradeEvent({ event: "entry_placed", ... })` (line 504).

The BUY order itself, though, was already live at the broker. It filled on its own sometime after 9:31am, becoming a **real, naked position with zero local tracking and zero resting stop-loss** — `enterLong()`'s thrown error was caught upstream and logged as `[risk] enterLong failed for NVTS: ...`, and the process just moved on as if nothing had opened. Nothing in `main.ts`/`orderManager.ts` re-checks for an orphaned fill after a failed `enterLong()` call.

**It stayed that way for the rest of the session** — confirmed directly: no further NVTS stop/entry log lines appear anywhere in today's run log between 9:31am and the EOD close. The only reason it got closed at all is that `closeAllEndOfDay()` queries the broker fresh at 3:20pm regardless of what's in the in-memory position map, found the orphaned NVTS position, and flattened it at 12.42 for **-$54.00**. The `holdMinutes: 0` and `volume: null` on the logged exit are the signature of exactly this: a position `closeAllEndOfDay()` discovered cold, with no local `entryTime` or volume history ever recorded for it.

**This is a real risk-management gap, not just a logging gap.** For roughly 6 hours, a real 300-share position had no stop-loss resting at all. Today it drifted in NVTS's favor for a while (seen in the 13:31–19:19 UTC bar log peaking around 12.76) before giving it back to 12.42 by the close — the $54 loss was mild by chance. Had NVTS moved sharply against the position at any point in that window, there was nothing in place to limit the damage; the strategy's entire hard-stop/breakeven/trailing design was simply absent for this position the whole time it existed.

**This will recur** any time `pollOrderFill()` times out on the entry *and* the subsequent stop-order placement fails for any reason (this exact "order already pending" collision, or any other transient API error) — it isn't specific to today's particular 417.

## Suggested fix (not applied — flagging for a decision)

`enterLong()` needs to either:
1. **Log `entry_placed` as soon as the entry order is confirmed placed** (before attempting the stop), so a subsequent stop-placement failure still leaves an audit trail and lets `this.positions` track it — even if the stop itself needs a retry — rather than treating the whole sequence as atomic; or
2. **Retry the stop placement** (similar to the existing `cancelOrderWithRetry` pattern used elsewhere for a different timing race) instead of throwing immediately on the first 417; or
3. At minimum, **wrap the stop placement in try/catch** and on failure, still register the position locally and log the entry (flagged as "unprotected"), so at least `checkForFills()`'s per-minute poll and the EOD close both know about it immediately rather than discovering it cold hours later.
