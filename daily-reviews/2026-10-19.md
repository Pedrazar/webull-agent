## Flag: stale `missed_entry` for OPK with a 3-day-old `barTime`

The 13:30:13 `missed_entry` event cites a bar timestamped `2026-10-16T19:58:00Z` — Friday's close, not any bar from today's session. Per `CLAUDE.md`'s documented `seedSymbol()` design, bars from before today's 9:30am ET open should go through *silent* `seed()` (EMA warmup only, no replay), and only bars at-or-after today's open should be replayed through `onBarClose()` (which is what actually produces a `missed_entry` log line). A Friday-afternoon bar being replayed as if it were a live/current signal suggests the pre-open/post-open split may be comparing time-of-day only rather than date+time — i.e. 19:58 UTC looks like "after 9:30am ET" in isolation even though it's the wrong calendar day. No harm done this time (correctly never auto-traded — `missed_entry` is log-only by design), but worth a code-level look since the same mechanism gates whether genuine pre-market gaps get caught correctly.
